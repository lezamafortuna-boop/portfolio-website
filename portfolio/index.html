About Me

A unique blend of technology, creativity, and resilience — shaped by a life lived across Mexico and Canada.

My path through education and career has been anything but conventional—and I wouldn't have it any other way. It all began when I was just 12 years old in Mexico, where I enrolled in a Computer Systems Technician program. By the time I was 15, I had completed the course, laying the groundwork for a lifelong interest in technology.

In 2006, I earned my degree in Computer Science with a focus on Information Systems. During my university years, I worked in the IT department of the Mexican government, gaining hands-on experience in public sector technology and administration.

In 2007, I moved to Vancouver, Canada, initially to study English. That journey led me to explore biochemistry at UBC, and later, a deep passion for the culinary arts. I completed Professional Cook Levels 1–3 and earned my Red Seal Certification in 2021.

Now, I'm channeling my diverse background into software development — building projects that combine creativity, logic, and a lifelong love of learning.
