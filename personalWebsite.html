My Journey

An Unconventional Path

From a computer lab in Mexico to a professional kitchen in Vancouver — every chapter shaped the developer I'm becoming.

2002 – 2006

Computer Science Degree

Mexico · Informática Administrativa

First program written at age 12. By 15, completed a full Computer Systems Technician course. Earned a CS degree in 2006 focused on Information Systems and worked in the IT department of the Mexican government from 2003–2006.

2007 – 2017

Vancouver & UBC

Vancouver, BC · University of British Columbia

Arrived for a 6-month English program — and never left. Enrolled at UBC in Biochemistry, discovering a love for scientific thinking and challenging myself in a completely new intellectual domain before pivoting once more.

2018 – 2021

Red Seal Certification

Vancouver · PICA & Vancouver Community College

A bold pivot into professional cooking. Pacific Institute of Culinary Arts → VCC apprenticeship → Professional Cook Levels 1, 2 & 3. Earned the Interprovincial Red Seal Certification in February 2021 — a nationally recognized credential validating culinary mastery.

Now · 2025

Software Developer

Vancouver, BC · CircuitStream

Bringing it all together. A software development student channeling 20+ years of technical background, scientific curiosity, and culinary discipline into building modern web and mobile applications — one clean commit at a time.
