About me

Hi, I'm Luis — a software development student exploring the world of web design and coding.

Who am I?

My path through education and career has been anything but conventional—and I wouldn't have it any other way. It all began when I was just 12 years old in Mexico, where I enrolled in a Computer Systems Technician program. By the time I was 15, I had completed the course, laying the groundwork for a lifelong interest in technology. After finishing high school in 2002, I added a diploma in specialized accounting training to my toolkit, further expanding my understanding of systems and data.


In 2006, I earned my degree in Computer Science with a focus on Information Systems. During my university years, I worked in the IT department of the Mexican government from 2003 to 2006, gaining hands-on experience in public sector technology and administration.


In 2007, I made a bold move to Vancouver, Canada, initially to study English for six months. That short-term plan evolved into a deeper exploration of new possibilities. I enrolled at the University of British Columbia (UBC) to pursue biochemistry, driven by a curiosity for science and a desire to challenge myself in a completely different field. Although I left the program in my second year in 2017, the experience taught me resilience and broadened my perspective.


In 2018, I took a leap into the culinary world, enrolling in the Professional Cook Level 1 program at the Pacific Institute of Culinary Arts in Vancouver, BC. That experience ignited a deep passion for cooking and led me to pursue further training through the apprenticeship pathway at Vancouver Community College. I completed Professional Cook Level 2 in 2019 and continued on to Level 3, earning the Interprovincial Red Seal Certification in February 2021. This nationally recognized credential marked a major milestone in my culinary career, validating my skills and dedication to the craft. The journey through culinary school was intense, creative, and deeply rewarding—an exploration of flavor, discipline, and artistry that shaped me both professionally and personally.


Now, I'm channeling my diverse background and experiences into a new chapter as a software development student. This website is part of my journey to build new skills, tackle challenges, and grow into a confident developer. I believe in learning by doing, and I'm excited to create projects that reflect both creativity and logic.
